How setup works

  1. Enter the branded subdomain you want to use.
  2. Celebration Suite generates a TXT verification record immediately.
  3. Add that TXT record at your DNS provider.
  4. Point the subdomain to Celebration Suite using your DNS provider.
  5. Return here and click Verify & Activate.
For safest setup, use a subdomain such as portal.example.com rather than replacing your main marketing website.
