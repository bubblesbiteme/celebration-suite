# Celebration Suite — Luxury Feature Roadmap

A premium visual system raises trust. A platform that feels worth $10,000 must also remove meaningful labor, prevent expensive mistakes and create operating leverage. The following roadmap is prioritized by user value rather than novelty.

## Tier 1 — strongest value multipliers

### 1. Concierge Event Blueprint Wizard

**Audience:** DIY and professional

Collect event type, date, guest count, budget, location, venue status, service level, food style, design direction and accessibility needs. Generate a complete starting workspace:

- tailored checklist;
- timeline with recommended due dates;
- starter budget and category targets;
- questionnaire set;
- guest and invitation milestones;
- design brief;
- shopping/rental gap list;
- starter floor-plan suggestion;
- event-day outline.

**Why it matters:** It turns a complex platform into an immediate result and materially reduces setup labor.

### 2. Intelligent Budget, Quantity and Procurement Engine

**Audience:** DIY and professional

Connect guest count, floor plan, menu, inventory, design selections, vendor quotes and rental availability to calculate:

- recommended quantities;
- owned inventory coverage;
- purchase and rental gaps;
- tax, delivery, service and contingency costs;
- budget overage warnings;
- per-guest and per-table costs;
- shopping, packing and return lists;
- client decor allowance versus planner service revenue.

**Why it matters:** Preventing over-ordering, missing quantities and hidden fees creates measurable financial value.

### 3. Reusable Template Studio

**Audience:** professional, with guided packs for DIY

Allow users to save and reuse:

- event blueprints;
- checklists and timelines;
- questionnaires;
- proposals and agreements;
- payment schedules;
- client email/SMS sequences;
- floor-plan starters;
- run-of-show templates;
- Event Book layouts;
- automation recipes.

Add private organization libraries first; add an optional creator marketplace later.

**Why it matters:** It turns planner knowledge into repeatable intellectual property and reduces setup time for every future event.

### 4. Client Journey Orchestration

**Audience:** professional

Add a visual journey builder for inquiry through closeout:

- milestone-triggered branded emails and SMS;
- next-action prompts;
- questionnaire and approval reminders;
- payment and document sequences;
- automated client progress digests;
- post-event review/referral requests;
- escalation when a client is stalled.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest ways to make the product feel white-glove while reducing manual follow-up.

### 5. Offline Event-Day Command Mode

**Audience:** professional and serious DIY

Cache critical information locally:

- run of show;
- vendor and emergency contacts;
- guest/check-in data;
- staff assignments;
- floor plan;
- packing/load lists;
- incident log;
- last synchronized changes.

Queue updates and reconcile when connectivity returns.

**Why it matters:** Event venues often have unreliable service. Operational continuity is a premium reliability feature.

## Tier 2 — professional operating leverage

### 6. Resource Capacity and Conflict Forecasting

Forecast inventory, staff, vehicles, warehouse pulls, vendor dependencies and event overlap across future dates. Surface conflicts before a proposal is accepted.

### 7. Profitability and Scenario Intelligence

Add event contribution margin, labor utilization, inventory depreciation, vendor markup, decor allowance consumption, cash-flow timing and best/worst-case scenario forecasts.

### 8. Vendor Risk and Compliance Center

Track insurance certificates, licenses, W-9s, agreements, expiration dates, service issues, arrival performance, cancellation risk and preferred-vendor standing.

### 9. Multi-brand / Multi-location Agency Support

Support multiple business brands, teams, inventories, regions and client-facing identities under one owner account with consolidated reporting.

### 10. Advanced Approval and Version Proofing

Add side-by-side versions, annotation pins, structured client decisions, sign-off history, revision limits and approval dependencies for design, floor plans, documents and budgets.

## Tier 3 — premium expansion

### 11. Household and Committee Collaborator Roles

Add partner, parent, committee member, assistant and venue-contact roles with limited access, attributed activity and approval permissions.

### 12. Executive Client Experience

Offer branded milestone summaries, a premium mobile home screen, concierge chat, timeline digest, decision queue, travel/venue information and a final digital Event Book.

### 13. Portfolio Intelligence

Turn closed events into a searchable internal knowledge base with actual costs, quantities, floor-plan performance, vendor outcomes, client feedback and reusable lessons.

### 14. Template Marketplace

Allow verified professionals to sell curated event templates, questionnaires, workflows, design packs and operational systems. This should follow strong private template tooling, not precede it.

## Recommended sequence

1. Event Blueprint Wizard
2. Budget/Quantity/Procurement Engine
3. Reusable Template Studio
4. Client Journey Orchestration
5. Offline Event-Day Mode
6. Resource Forecasting and Profitability
7. Vendor Compliance
8. Multi-brand Agency Support
9. Version Proofing
10. Marketplace

## Product-positioning recommendation

Do not market the platform as expensive because it has many screens. Position it as premium because it provides:

- a client experience that protects the planner's brand;
- an operating system that reduces administrative labor;
- controls that prevent event-day and financial mistakes;
- reusable intellectual property;
- professional visibility from inquiry through closeout;
- a guided experience for people planning one important celebration.
