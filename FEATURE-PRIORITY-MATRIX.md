## Best next investments

### P1 — strongest commercial and usability return

1. **Guided Event Setup Wizard and template library**  
   Ask event type, date, guest count, budget, venue status, and planning level; then create a tailored checklist, timeline, budget categories, questionnaire, floor-plan starter, and shopping/rental list. This is the most important bridge for true DIY users.

2. **Smart Budget and Shopping List**  
   Convert design-board selections, guest count, menu choices, inventory availability, rentals, and vendor quotes into quantities, estimated spend, purchase/rental gaps, and a printable shopping/load list.

3. **Template Marketplace / Agency Template Packs**  
   Let professionals save and sell or share reusable workflows, questionnaires, proposals, timelines, run-of-show templates, floor-plan starters, email sequences, and package configurations.

4. **Household and Co-planner Collaboration**  
   Add simple collaborator roles such as partner, parent, committee member, assistant, and venue contact with module-level permissions and change attribution.

5. **Offline Event-Day Mode**  
   Cache the run of show, contacts, guest check-in list, staff assignments, inventory load sheets, emergency notes, and recent changes for weak venue connectivity.

### P2 — valuable differentiators after launch stability

- Inspiration-to-budget estimate and alternative recommendations.
- Menu/serving, beverage, seating and linen quantity calculators.
- Weather and contingency-plan workspace.
- Accessibility and guest-accommodation planning prompts.
- Vendor insurance/COI expiration tracking.
- Client satisfaction/NPS and referral automation.
- Portfolio builder that converts approved post-event media into a branded case study.
- Multi-brand or multi-location agency workspaces.

## Features not recommended before launch

- A broad public vendor marketplace requiring marketplace payouts and dispute handling.
- Native mobile apps before the responsive/PWA flow has real beta usage data.
- More AI generation surfaces before current AI actions have approval, audit and cost controls validated in production.
- Social-media publishing as a core module; integrations are lower risk than owning a publishing stack.
