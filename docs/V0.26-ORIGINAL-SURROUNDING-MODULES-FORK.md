# Celebration Suite v0.26.0 — Original Surrounding Modules Fork

This release continues the architectural reset started in v0.25.0. Rather than recreating Sage & Celebration workflows in a separate UI, the original working planner surfaces are reused and adapted at the server boundary for tenant isolation.

## Direct-fork surfaces in this round

- Guest Management (`guests-admin.html`)
- Scheduling & appointment administration (`scheduling-admin.html`)
- Event Day Command Center (`event-day-command.html`)

## Compatibility work

- `guest-admin.mjs` now exposes the original guest-management contract over tenant-scoped event, guest, RSVP, and ticket data.
- `appointment-scheduling.mjs` now supports the original appointment-type, availability, blackout, and appointment-status actions while preserving event-specific access checks.
- `google-calendar-auth.mjs` and `google-calendar-admin.mjs` provide the original scheduling page with tenant-aware Google Calendar compatibility.
- Original admin/dashboard links on the forked pages now return to Celebration Suite rather than Sage-only routes.

## Audit findings still queued

The remaining highest-priority direct-fork conversions are Financial Center, Vendor CRM, Communications Center, Team administration, Integrations/API management, QuickBooks administration, and the fuller original Client Portal surface. Those remain on their currently working Celebration Suite implementations until their compatibility adapters are ready.
