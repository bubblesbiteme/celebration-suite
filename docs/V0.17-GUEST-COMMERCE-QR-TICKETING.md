# v0.17 Guest Commerce, QR Ticketing & Unified Check-In

Celebration Suite v0.17 deepens guest operations into one connected system.

## Guest & RSVP QR
- General RSVP QR for printed invitations/signage.
- Personalized guest QR codes using `CSGUEST:<code>`.
- RSVP confirmation can display the guest's check-in QR.
- Guest Suite shows RSVP guests and ticket holders together.

## Ticket sales
- Event-level public ticket-sales page.
- Multiple free/paid ticket types with descriptions and capacities.
- Sale close time, overall event capacity, phone/organization options.
- Promo codes: percentage or fixed discount, optional expiry/use limits.
- Multi-ticket orders with one attendee record per ticket.
- Stripe Checkout for paid tickets; free tickets complete instantly.
- Order cancellation and Stripe refunds from the planner interface.

## Ticket QR
- Every attendee gets a unique `TKT-...` code.
- Public ticket wallet renders a QR payload `CSTICKET:<ticket_code>`.
- Tickets can be printed or saved individually.

## Unified QR check-in
- One mobile scanner accepts RSVP guest and ticket QR codes.
- Camera scanning via html5-qrcode plus manual/paste fallback.
- Search and manual check-in remain available.
- Event-day workspace links directly to the scanner.
