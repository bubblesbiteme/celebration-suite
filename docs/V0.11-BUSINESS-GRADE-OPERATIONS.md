# Celebration Suite v0.11.0 — Business-Grade Operations

Adds planner-side operational depth for commercial use:

- Event Day Command Center tasks, staff-on-clock view, incident log, guest/registration check-in, vendors and reserved inventory.
- Purchasing & Rental Gap Center for purchase/rental/borrow needs, source links, cost estimates and receiving status.
- Vendor Reviews with private post-event ratings and rehire history.
- Referral Partner Access with tokenized read-only commission portals and link revocation.
- Accounting Reconciliation Center comparing Celebration Suite billed/outstanding totals with QuickBooks Online invoice/open-balance totals, with saved reconciliation snapshots.
- Backup/export coverage for the new operational records.

Database migration: `0012_BUSINESS_GRADE_OPERATIONS.sql`.
