# v0.32 Unified White-Label Branding & Integration

This release makes the tenant brand record the single source of truth across planner and client-facing surfaces.

## Branding behavior
- Custom planner logo is used when uploaded.
- Celebration Suite logo is the default when no custom logo is present.
- Primary, Secondary and Accent colors propagate through the planner shell, original direct-fork pages, client portal and public client links.
- Legacy Sage & Celebration headers/logos are replaced at runtime on authenticated tenant pages.

## Brand settings
- Direct logo upload (PNG/JPG/WEBP/GIF/SVG, 3 MB max)
- Live preview
- Celebration Suite default reset
- Business name, primary, secondary and accent colors
- Email sender and reply-to identity

## Client-facing branded surfaces
- Client portal and login
- My Events
- RSVP/invitations
- Ticket sales and ticket wallet
- Proposals
- Questionnaires
- Approvals and change orders
- Invoice payment page
- Tenant-sent email wrapper

## Payment integration
A missing public invoice endpoint was restored. Public invoice pages use the tenant brand and create Stripe Checkout using the tenant's connected Stripe account.

No database migration is required.
