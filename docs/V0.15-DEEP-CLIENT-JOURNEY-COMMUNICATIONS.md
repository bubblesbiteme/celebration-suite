# v0.15 Deep Client Journey & Communications

## Added
- Scheduling Settings with client-bookable appointment types, durations, meeting modes, buffers, weekly availability and blackout periods.
- Client Portal Appointments with availability-derived booking slots and 24-hour cancellation protection.
- Vendor Quote Comparison with true-cost calculations, recommended/client-visible flags, validity dates, deposits and Select & Book conversion into vendor + event budget records.
- Client-facing Vendor Options view for quotes explicitly shared by the planner.
- Unified Communications upgraded for inbound replies, unmatched/ambiguous resolution, contextual Reply-To routing and provider metadata.
- Resend inbound email webhook and Telnyx inbound SMS webhook receivers.
- Lead CRM depth: temperature, tags, consultation timestamps, lost reasons, overdue follow-up metrics and communications integration.
- Automation rules for agreement signed, invoice due soon, questionnaire follow-up, overdue tasks and vendor-document expiry.
