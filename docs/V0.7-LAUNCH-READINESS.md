# Celebration Suite v0.7 — Launch Readiness

## Brand refresh
- Replaces the original abstract checklist mark with the approved elegant Celebration Suite identity.
- Shared logo, compact mark and favicon assets update platform-owned branding across the site without overriding tenant white-label branding.

## Checkout readiness and diagnostics
- `checkout-readiness` reports whether the Stripe secret and each of the three platform Price IDs are present.
- `activate.html` checks readiness before purchase and disables only the unavailable plan button(s).
- `checkout-create` now identifies which Stripe environment variable is missing without exposing its value.

## Platform transactional email
Celebration Suite now supports platform-owned transactional email through Resend.
Required:
- `RESEND_API_KEY`
- `PLATFORM_FROM_EMAIL` (recommended: `support@sageandcelebration.com`)
- `PLATFORM_FROM_NAME` (recommended: `Celebration Suite`)

Tenant/planner client email remains separate and continues to use each tenant's configured Resend/SMTP credentials.

## Password reset
- Planner/team accounts
- Platform owner/admin accounts
- Client accounts scoped to their portal/event invitation
- Reset tokens are one-time, hashed at rest and expire after 24 hours.
- Reset requests do not disclose whether an email address exists.

Run migration:
`supabase/migrations/0008_LAUNCH_EMAIL_PASSWORD_RESETS.sql`

## Launch Health
Platform admins can open `/launch-health.html` to verify configuration readiness for:
- site URL
- Supabase connection and server key
- session/encryption secrets
- Stripe secret, 3 plan prices and webhook signing secret
- Google OAuth credentials/state protection
- transactional email
- support email

No credential values are returned to the browser.
