# Celebration Suite v0.7.3 — Annual Subscription Hotfix

## Fixes
- Annual Unlimited now opens Stripe Checkout in recurring `subscription` mode, matching a recurring yearly Stripe Price.
- Both Monthly Unlimited and Annual Unlimited derive their renewal/expiration date from Stripe subscription period data.
- Both recurring plans store Stripe subscription/customer IDs and current period end.
- Stripe webhook renewal, failed-payment recovery, cancellation, and reactivation logic works for both recurring plans through the stored subscription ID.
- Switching between Monthly and Annual cancels the previous Stripe subscription after the replacement subscription is verified, preventing duplicate recurring charges.
- Single Event remains one-time `payment` mode.

No Supabase migration is required.
