A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Clear access, no guesswork.

Can a DIY planner use the professional tools later?

Yes. Guided Planning keeps advanced tools out of the way, but they remain available whenever the event grows more complex.

Will clients see Celebration Suite branding?

Planner-facing product branding and client-facing white-label branding are separate. Your clients see the business identity configured for your workspace.

Can I change plans?

Plan changes and renewal options are managed from Billing. Access and account-recovery terms are shown before a change takes effect.

Does the platform replace my payment processor?

No. Professional planners can connect their own supported payment account so client transactions flow through their business configuration.
